This is a design of a test result screen that consists of 2 separate cards that are joined together within the <main> tag.
Both cards have <section> tag and are used for separate tasks.
The left card shows the average or overall score and the card on the right shows the results in separate tests.
The boxes with individual test results are in the form of an unordered list.

Responsiveness was maintained in the design by ensuring that I use minimum hardcoded lengths and widths. I applied this concept by giving no height or width to the body tag or the main tag, although the main tag has a width but it's optimized by the use of both width and max-width so that the size can be shortened or lengthened according to the screen size of the users' device.

The button used in the right section supports hovering and focus-visible effects to create a stunning design and offer support to users who might navigate the website using their keyboard.

The sizes of elements were given by pure intuition to ensure they look as close to the target design as possible. By doing all this I ensured that the design is as responsive, accurate and precise as possible.
